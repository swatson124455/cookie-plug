How it works

  1. You send the {{ site.cfg.wording.check_name }}, or call. {{ site.form.summary }}.
  2. We confirm fit with the facility: line, format, volume, and timing. If it is not a fit, we say so plainly, and if we know where you should look instead, we tell you.
  3. You send your product, recipe, or spec for a sample and judge it against your own product or your brief.
  4. The facility quotes in writing once you approve the sample.
  5. You sign directly with the facility. From then on you work with the plant, and we stay reachable if anything snags.

Who pays us

The facility pays us a share of what its customers buy, the way many manufacturers pay brokers and sales representatives. Brands never pay us anything: no retainer, no finder's fee, no invoice from us.

Why the facility is not named on this site

This site is ours, not the plant's, so it states only what the facility has confirmed in writing for publication. Before anything is produced, you will know exactly who makes your product, see its documents, and sign with it directly.

What we will never claim

If a certification, a minimum, or a lead time is not on this site, it has not been confirmed for publication yet. Ask, and we get it confirmed for your product, in writing, before you plan around it. We would rather lose a lead than win one with a number that turns out to be wrong.
