# Facility Data Sheet and Referral Terms

Send sections 1 to 3 to the partner in week 1. Their answers go into `config/facility.yaml`; run `leadgen facility-check` until nothing is marked `TO_CONFIRM`. Section 4 is the referral agreement you need signed before the first handoff. Nothing in outreach may claim a fact that is not on this sheet.

## 1. Production capability

| Question | Why buyers ask |
|---|---|
| Which lines run today: drop cookies, wire-cut, rotary molded, bars, brownies, muffins, crackers, extruded pet kibble, baked pet biscuits, soft chews, jerky? | Decides which leads are even possible |
| Monthly capacity per line in units and in pounds, and how much is open | Buyers size their forecast against it |
| Minimum run size per line (units, pounds, or dollars) | The first qualifying question on every call |
| Packaging formats: flow wrap, stand-up pouch, tray, box, bulk, club pack | Retail buyers require specific formats |
| Can you run allergen-free (nut-free, gluten-free) with segregation or dedicated lines? | Gates a large share of the cookie market |
| Shelf-life capability and testing available in-house | Needed for retail specs |
| Cold chain or frozen? | Rules certain products in or out |

## 2. Certifications and compliance (attach certificates)

- FDA registration number and last inspection date
- GFSI scheme and level: SQF, BRC, or FSSC 22000, with certificate and expiry
- Organic certifier (if any)
- Gluten-free certification (GFCO or other)
- Kosher and halal
- Non-GMO Project
- AAFCO compliance for pet food and treats, state feed registrations held
- Nutrition-panel and label-compliance services: who does the work, turnaround
- Recall plan and insurance certificate (product liability limits)

## 4. Referral agreement: terms to get in writing

1. **Definition of a referred account:** any company you introduce, or that you logged in your pipeline before the facility's first contact. Attach your weekly export as the register.
2. **Commission:** the percentage of net invoiced purchases, including repurchases and new SKUs from the same account.
3. **Term:** how many months from the account's first PO the commission runs (you said roughly two years; write the number).
4. **Payment timing:** monthly, within 30 days of the customer's payment, with a statement listing invoices.
5. **Audit right:** you may review invoices for referred accounts once a quarter.
6. **Account transfer:** what happens if the account moves to an affiliate of the facility or the facility is sold.
7. **Non-circumvention:** the facility will not route a referred account through another entity to avoid commission.
8. **Exclusivity:** none on your side (you confirmed), and no obligation for the facility to accept every lead. Decline reasons are shared with you.
9. **Marketing use:** what you may say about the facility, which name to use, and who approves claims.
10. **Termination:** either side on 30 days' notice; commissions on existing referred accounts survive termination for the full term.

## 5. Capabilities one-pager (build it after section 1 to 3 are answered)

One page, PDF, sent after the first reply. Contents: lines and categories, open capacity statement, turnkey services list, certifications with logos, MOQ and lead-time ranges, sample process in three steps, a contact line for you. No pricing on the page; pricing goes in a conversation.
