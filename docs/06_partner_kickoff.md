## 3. Handoff protocol (agree it on the call)

1. Referrer emails an intro with the buyer's need, volume, timing, and requirements, and copies Facility's named contact.
2. Facility responds to the buyer within one business day and copies Referrer on every thread.
3. Samples ship within the turnaround on the data sheet; Referrer chases feedback.
4. Facility sends pricing to the buyer and Referrer at the same time.
5. Facility tells Referrer when the PO signs and when the first run ships, and adds the account to the commission statement.

## 4. Weekly reporting you will send them

Every Friday: `leadgen export data/pipeline_YYYYMMDD.csv` and `leadgen report`. Email both to the partner and to yourself. That email thread is the register that defines Referred Accounts.
