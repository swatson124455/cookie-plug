# Inbound Demand: Where Brands Are Already Asking for a Co-Packer

Outbound finds brands before they know they need you. Inbound catches the ones already looking, which is the easiest win in this whole plan and costs nothing but a profile and a weekly check. Do these in week 1.

## 1. List the facility on the co-manufacturer marketplaces (free tier)

| Platform | What it is | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Keychain (keychain.com) | The largest CPG manufacturing matchmaker, 28,000+ brands and manufacturers, backed by General Mills and others. Brands and retailers search by process and capability; free base listing, paid premium profile. | Create the facility's profile with every line, process, format, and certification from the data sheet. Set you as the contact for inbound. Check requests twice a week. |
| PartnerSlate (partnerslate.com) | Ten-year-old matching platform. Brands post projects anonymously and the matching AI alerts compatible co-mans; 553 bakery and 577 snack manufacturers listed. | Register as a supplier (partnerslate.com/suppliers). Respond to every matched project within 24 hours; speed wins these. |
| Specialty Food Association co-packer directory, Snack Food & Wholesale Bakery directory, Pet Food Processing supplier directory | Trade-association listings buyers browse | Free or low-cost listings; fill them once. |

**Pin:** these profiles have to be created by, or with, the facility, and the referral agreement must say marketplace inbound that you handle counts as referred. Raise both on the kickoff call.

## 2. Answer the "looking for a co-packer" posts

- **Startup CPG Slack** (startupcpg.com/join, free, 22,000 members): founders post co-packer asks weekly. Join, watch the co-packer and manufacturing channels, and answer in public with one useful sentence and a DM offer. Being helpful in public is the pitch.
- **Foodbevy** (paid community, about $360 a year): same pattern, smaller and more established founders. Defer to Phase 1.
- **Cottage-food and home-baker groups** on Facebook (Cottage Food Business and similar; dog-treat business groups): thousands of home bakers, and the recurring post is "I can't legally sell dog treats from my kitchen" or "my state won't let me sell pet treats without a licensed facility." That is Tier B demand in its rawest form. Answer with the licensed-facility path; qualify hard on volume.
- **Reddit**: r/foodindustry, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur for "co-packer" threads. Low volume, high intent.

Template reply: "Depends on your volume and format. Under about [MOQ] units a run most co-packers won't take it; above that, a plant with open capacity can usually sample in a few weeks. Happy to tell you whether your product fits, no pitch. DM me the product and rough monthly units."

## 3. Ask the facility for the leads it already has

Every plant turns away inquiries and has old quotes that went quiet. Ask on the kickoff call for:
- Inbound inquiries from the past 24 months that never closed, with the reason.
- Quotes issued but not signed.
- Former customers who left, and why.
- Their ingredient, packaging, and label suppliers' reps, who talk to other brands daily and will make introductions for a share of the referral.

Import them with `source=facility_inbound`. These are the warmest leads you will ever get and the fastest path to the proof gate. Confirm in the agreement how re-engaged facility leads are credited.

## 4. Reshoring: brands importing what the facility could make

Imported treats and biscuits carry tariff and freight risk in 2026. Brands whose packaging or site says "made in Germany", "product of China", "imported from Italy" in cookies, shortbread, or meat treats are candidates for a US second source. Butcher's Naturals (German-made) is already in the list for this reason. Search: `"dog treats" "made in Germany" OR "product of China" site:*.com`, `"shortbread" "imported from" brand`.

## 5. Make you findable

- LinkedIn headline: "I help cookie, bakery and pet-treat brands find open US co-packing capacity." Two posts a week (see the playbook). Founders search LinkedIn for exactly this phrase.
- A one-page site or even a Notion page with the capabilities summary and a contact form, once the data sheet is complete. Not before: an empty capabilities page loses the lead.

## Weekly inbound routine (30 minutes)

Monday: check Keychain and PartnerSlate requests, reply to all. Wednesday: scan Startup CPG and the Facebook groups, answer two posts. Friday: log every inbound conversation as a lead with `source=inbound_<platform>`.
