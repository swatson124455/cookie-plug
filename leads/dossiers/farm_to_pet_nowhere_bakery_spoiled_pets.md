# Account Dossiers E (WebSearch only, 2026-09-29)

## Farm to Pet

- **Product line:** Single-ingredient dehydrated chips: Chicken Chips (100% chicken breast), Turkey Chips, Beef Chips (beef round), Fish Chips; plus dog food toppers, cat treats, antler chews, snack packs (https://farmtopettreats.com/collections/shop-all). Chicken Chips in 4 oz and 12 oz (https://www.dogdogcat.com/products/farm-to-pet-chicken-chips-natural-dog-treats-single-ingredient-usa-made-4-oz-12-oz). Claims: human-grade, USA-made, cage-free/certified humane chicken from Iowa farms, no additives (https://farmtopettreats.com/products/chicken-chips). Shelf-stable status: unknown.
- **Where sold:** DTC subscription, Amazon storefront (https://www.amazon.com/stores/FarmToPet/page/4BF372F2-73E2-4FFF-BC32-CD7C31D4AC05), Walmart.com, 650 retail partners per press release (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260626770345/en/Profitable-Pet-Brand-Farm-to-Pet-Launches-Crowdfunding-Campaign-to-Fuel-Next-Phase-of-Growth); "more than 1,000 independent retail locations," no distributors by design (https://farmtopettreats.com/blogs/word-on-the-treats/jackson-jones-talks-building-farm-to-pet-on-the-pre-ipo-podcast). Chewy: unknown.
- **Who makes it today:** Own 13,000 sq ft Chicago facility; vertically integrated (receives, produces, packages, ships); explicitly chose not to use co-packers (https://farmtopettreats.com/blogs/word-on-the-treats/why-us-made-dog-treats-matter-farm-to-pets-new-chicago-facility).
- **What is changing:** June 26, 2026 StartEngine Reg CF ($20K min, $1.24M max, $24M valuation) to "expand production capacity," wholesale expansion, product development (https://www.petfoodindustry.com/news-newsletters/pet-food-news/news/15828901/farm-to-pet-opens-first-crowdfunding-campaign-on-startengine; https://kingscrowd.com/farm-to-pet-on-startengine-2026/). Investor page: "4X larger factory with higher output equipment," automation at bottlenecks; Jones recently invested in a new facility and machinery to "more than double manufacturing capacity"; overtime shifts straining cash flow (https://invest.investfarmtopet.com/). Raised only $16,357 at last snapshot (https://www.findcrowdfunding.com/en/view/startengine/farm-to-pet).
- **Scale signals:** $80K (2021) to $5M+ (2025), 186% CAGR, profitable 2026, 100K+ orders/yr, 250K customers, bootstrapped (Business Wire above). Headcount: unknown.
- **People:** Jackson Jones, Founder & CEO (https://www.linkedin.com/in/jacksonajones/); Jacki Jimenez Benitez, operations support; Mary Edler, special projects (https://farmtopettreats.com/pages/our-team).
- **Leadership statements:** "You can't go too fast because you're limited by the money you have, inventory you can buy and equipment you can buy" (https://www.superpowers4good.com/p/from-dog-treats-to-crowdfunding-farm). "To date, we've been self-funded" (PetfoodIndustry above).
- **Proposed give:** Overflow-capacity plan for Chicken Chips 4 oz during the factory move, plus a baked treat line extension concept (their product development line item).
- **Risks:** High. Building own 4X plant; public anti-co-packer stance; product is ground-and-dehydrated meat, not baked. A baker with dehydrators/low-temp ovens could plausibly make a similar chip, but it is not their core. Best angle is bridge capacity or a new baked SKU, not core chips.

## Nowhere Bakery

- **Product line:** Individually wrapped cookies, brownies, blondies; gluten-free, grain-free, dairy/egg/soy-free, no cane sugar (coconut sugar, maple) (https://startupcpg.com/newswire/startup-cpg-newswire-nowhere-bakery-launches-in-sprouts-across-california). Packs: Chocolate Chip 12/24, Double Choc 6/12/24, N'oatmeal 5/10/20 (https://nowherebakery.com/products/chocolate-chip-cookies). Not shelf-stable: 30 days refrigerated, 6 months frozen; GF certification "in process," dedicated GF kitchen (https://nowherebakery.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions). Snackies (bite-size, from trimmings) beta 2025, wholesale by July 2026 (https://nowherebakery.com/products/snackies; https://www.foodrepublic.com/1883894/nowhere-bakery-shark-tank-now/).
- **Where sold:** DTC nationwide, Amazon store, Good Eggs, Faire (https://www.faire.com/brand/b_7xpd8uf7ww); 100+ Sprouts stores in California from Aug 2024 (Startup CPG above); 75 stores incl. Sunlife Organics, Alfred Coffee, Foxtrot (Food Republic above). Wholesale about 35% of business (https://www.cnbc.com/2024/03/19/shark-tank-how-nowhere-bakery-struck-deal-with-barbara-corcoran.html).
- **Who makes it today:** Own commercial kitchen; production facility in Costa Mesa; "maintained in-house production" 2025 (https://sharktanksuccess.com/nowhere-bakery-update-after-shark-tank/). On Shark Tank they rejected co-packing to control quality (CNBC above). Yelp lists 3189 Airway Ave, Costa Mesa as CLOSED (Sept 2026) (https://www.yelp.com/biz/nowhere-bakery-costa-mesa); new site: unknown.
- **What is changing:** Channel shift from DTC/frozen to grocery (Sprouts, Aug 2024) and a new bite-size format (Snackies, 2025-2026). Rasti: biggest challenge is "scaling a manufacturing process without compromising quality" (https://itsallhappeningco.substack.com/p/reimagining-indulgence-saphira-rastis). Airway Ave closure suggests a facility move; unconfirmed.
- **Scale signals:** $92K, $524K, $770K ($32K profit) years 1-3, $1M projected; unit cost $1.92, sells $5.50-6 (CNBC above). Corcoran $200K for 12.5%. Valuation est. $1.94M (https://sharktanknetworth.com/nowhere-bakery-net-worth-shark-tank-update/). Headcount: unknown.
- **People:** Saphira Rasti, Co-Founder (https://www.linkedin.com/in/saphira-rasti-470911a9/); Maurizio Rasti, Co-Founder (CNBC above). Third contact: unknown; sales@nowherebakery.com.
- **Leadership statements:** "We're growing our presence in grocery stores like Sprouts" (Substack above); "we value controlling the quality" (CNBC above).
- **Proposed give:** Benchmark sample of a shelf-stable, GF/grain-free/vegan Chocolate Chip Snackie matching their coconut-sugar spec, with a capacity plan for Sprouts California.
- **Risks:** Small ($1M range); strong quality-control identity and on-record co-packer rejection; refrigerated/frozen product and pending GF certification complicate a shelf-stable conversion.

Claims: human-grade, cage-free chicken, wild-caught Alaskan salmon, no fillers. Pack sizes: unknown. Certifications: unknown.
- **Where sold:** DTC, Faire (https://www.faire.com/brand/b_bq29hr2psm), wholesale portal and intro pack (https://shopspoiledpets.com/products/sample-pack), "independent retail partners nationwide," pop-ups incl. Alpine TX; Chewy launch by end of July 2026, "largest retail expansion since launching in 2024" (https://www.petfoodprocessing.net/articles/20669-spoiled-pets-launches-on-chewy). Door counts: unknown.

- **What is changing:** Channel jump from DTC/Faire to Chewy (July 2026) plus new treats launched at SUPERZOO Aug 12-14, 2026 (Emerging Brands Pavilion, New Products Showcase), "connecting with retailers and distributors" (Pet Food Processing URLs above). Prior: won Startup CPG free booth at SUPERZOO 2025 (https://shopspoiledpets.com/blogs/the-spoiled-scoop/from-wedding-bells-to-superzoo-how-august-became-the-wildest-month-of-our-lives).
- **Scale signals:** Founded 2024; revenue, funding, headcount: unknown.
- **People:** Daedrien Leyva-Lynn, Founder & CEO (https://www.linkedin.com/in/daedrien); Brian Lynn, Co-Founder and COO (https://www.linkedin.com/in/brian-lynn-729145117/; https://shopspoiledpets.com/pages/about-us).

- **Proposed give:** A baked, shelf-stable cat or dog treat line extension (e.g., baked chicken-and-salmon biscuit) as a lower-cost companion SKU for Chewy, with a capacity plan.
- **Risks:** Core products are freeze-dried raw, not baked; a baker cannot make them as-is. Likely very small (2024 founding, pop-up sales). Upside: no named manufacturer, so no contract in the way.
