## Legally Addictive Foods

**Product line**
- Cracker cookies ("part cracker, all cookie"): cracker base, handmade salted toffee, Belgian semi-sweet chocolate, sea salt. Flavors seen: The O.G., Everything, Surprise Party, Churros, plus Spicewalla collabs (Chai Masala, Mexican Hot Chocolate, Salted Gingerbread); "six varieties plus two seasonal" / "8 varieties" (https://wncmagazine.com/feature/cookie_creativity; https://www.potatopro.com/companies/legally-addictive-foods).
- Pack: 4.7 oz bag, Amazon 2-packs; DTC "Party Pack of 4" (https://www.amazon.com/Legally-Addictive-Cracker-Cookies-Semi-Sweet/dp/B0784H82KK; https://legallyaddictivefoods.com/products/everything-cookies-unlike-everything-else-out-there). Described as shelf-stable (https://commercialbaking.com/legally-addictives-scrappy-entrepreneurial-journey/). Certifications: unknown; woman-owned claim only.

**Where sold**
- ~2,000 retail/hospitality partners incl. Whole Foods, The Fresh Market, Omni Hotels, Ritz-Carlton, JetBlue Mint (https://kingscrowd.com/legally-addictive-foods-on-vicinity-2026/). 43 Whole Foods (Southeast) (https://www.potatopro.com/companies/legally-addictive-foods). Amazon, DTC site, Faire wholesale (https://www.faire.com/brand/b_4duj5dlm), specialty e-tailers (Here Here Market, Martie, Snackathon). Distributor: unknown.

**Who makes it today**
- Self-manufactured: "currently manufactures its products in-house in North Carolina... maintaining the option to utilize qualified third-party manufacturing capacity where appropriate to supplement internal production" (Form C, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002142533/000182198226000029/LAFFormC.pdf). 7,000 sq ft Weaverville facility since Aug 2022 (https://wncmagazine.com/feature/cookie_creativity). Post-Helene: shut 10 days, lost product/accounts, then "doubled team and space" for JetBlue; no evidence of outsourcing (https://wlos.com/news/local/weaverville-cookie-cracker-maker-expands-after-landing-jetblue-mint-snack-deal-legally-addictive-foods-airline-snacks-optimist-ventures-legally-addictive-foods-business-hurricane-tropical-storm-helene-recovery). Kitchen Manager: Natalie Savage (https://www.linkedin.com/in/natalieksavage/).

**Scale signals**
- Team of 12 (Faire); $12M valuation; Reg CF $100K min / $1M max via Vicinity/StartEngine for "key hires, sales & marketing, production expansion, working capital" (Kingscrowd). Revenue: unknown.

**Trigger**
- Sept 2026: JetBlue Mint win + crowdfunding "to further scale production" and go national in grocery (WLOS, above; Kingscrowd 2026).

**People**
- Laura Shafferman, Founder/co-founder; Seth Eisman, co-founder / Partner (https://www.zoominfo.com/p/Seth-Eisman/565449017); Natalie Savage, Kitchen Manager.

**Leadership statements**
- Next goal is grocery expansion nationwide; scaling production is stated use of funds (WLOS; Form C).

**Proposed give**
- Free bench of a co-man-able, shelf-stable version of The O.G. (cracker/toffee/chocolate) with cost-per-4.7oz-bag model and a "surge/overflow" capacity plan so the $1M raise funds sales instead of ovens.

**Risks**
- Committed to in-house production and expanding its own space; small volumes; handmade toffee may be hard to replicate; raise may not close.

## YUMI

**Product line**
- Soft-baked functional cookies: Chunky Chocolate Chip and Double Chocolate Chip, 5.6 oz; sweet potato, oats, pea protein; 4g protein, 3–4g fiber, no refined sugar, gluten-free, nut-free, dairy-free (https://www.nosh.com/pr/2026/06/22/yumi-moves-into-lunchboxes-with-its-firstever-functional-cookie-launching-at-target-nationwide; https://www.target.com/p/yumi-chunky-chocolate-chip-cookies-5-6-oz/-/A-94882279). Also Organic Puffs (1.5 oz, rice-free), Organic toddler Bars, Organic Super Bar, pouches, six functional smoothies at Whole Foods; bars/puffs certified organic, "certified clean" (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/yumi-announces-nationwide-walmart-launch-as-retail-footprint-rapidly-expands-302136444.html; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/yumi-launches-six-functional-smoothies-at-whole-foods-nationwide-302702730.html). Shelf-stable vs frozen: unknown.

**Where sold**
- Target (since 2022; cookie exclusive), Walmart (May 2024), Whole Foods, Sprouts, Amazon, DTC, Faire; "0 to 11,300+ retail doors in just over three years" (https://www.linkedin.com/company/helloyumi).

**Who makes it today**
- Unknown. Baby meals were "made fresh every week in our LA kitchens"; snacks "made in USA"; no co-packer named (https://helloyumi.com/about/; https://helloyumi.com/).

**Scale signals**
- 28M+ bars sold; #1 toddler bar at Target (PR Newswire). $75–99M raised; 2021 valuation >$300M cut to ~$40M down round Jan 2024; 2023 revenue $13M (https://x.com/thebenbergman/status/1752827495085461799). ~65–66 employees (https://profiles.crustdata.com/company/yumi). Hiring Head of Sales for "next wave of national expansion" (LinkedIn).

**Trigger**
- June 22, 2026: first cookie launched at Target nationwide — a new baked category for a brand with no known bakery (NOSH, above).

**People**
- Angela Sutherland, co-founder/co-CEO; Evelyn Rusli, co-founder/co-CEO (also listed President/CMO); Arianna Schioldager, VP Content & Brand (https://www.linkedin.com/in/arianna-schioldager/).

**Leadership statements**
- Sutherland: "Most cookies in the functional aisle taste like a compromise" (NOSH). LinkedIn: "major announcements on the horizon in 2026."

**Proposed give**
- Gluten-free/nut-free/dairy-free soft-baked cookie benchmark against the 5.6 oz Target SKU with a second-source cost/capacity model for expansion beyond Target exclusivity.

**Risks**
- Cash-constrained post down-round; existing cookie co-man unknown and possibly locked; allergen (nut-free/GF) line requirements.

## Consumers Supply Distributing LLC

**Product line**
- Private-label dog biscuits: Country Vet Biscuits (Original Meaty Flavor 4 lb, UPC 899623000463; Multi Flavor 4 lb; Original Large 20 lb; Large Multi Flavor 20 lb at Menards) and Heartland Harvest Dog Biscuits Meaty Flavor with Chicken 4 lb (UPC 840227340691) (https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/consumers-supply-distributing-llc-recalls-country-vet-biscuits-dogs-due-possible-salmonella-health; https://www.menards.com/main/pet-wildlife/dog-supplies/dog-treats/country-vet-reg-large-multi-flavor-biscuit-dog-treats-20-lb/p1078/p-4340327767346152-c-8323.htm; https://www.wpvs.net/itemdetail/CV00048). Menards listing claims "Safe Feed/Safe Food facility," AAFCO all life stages. Also Heartland Harvest kibble (Bomgaars), Rancher's Choice trademark, CHS Payback/Equis feeds.
- Manufacturer of recalled biscuits: NOT named in FDA notice or any coverage seen; unknown. Company site says it has "two state-of-the-art manufacturing plants" and hires Production Operators to bag pet food (https://www.consumers-supply.com/our-story; https://www.ziprecruiter.com/c/Consumers-Supply-Distributing,-LLC/Job/Production-Operator/-in-Sioux-City,IA?jid=e643c9516b5191f6).

**Where sold**
- Farm/feed retail in CO, IA, KS, MO, TX, MT, NE (FDA); Bomgaars, Menards, Shipton's Big R, Saratoga Feed & Grain, WPVS, Pick Supply. Two distribution centers IA/SD.

**Who makes it today**
- Unknown (see above).

**Scale signals**
- ~$35M revenue, ~201 employees (Dec 2024) (https://leadiq.com/c/consumers-supply-distributing/5a1d91cf540000520076d5f1); $43.2M (Kona Equity). 9 open jobs in Sioux City incl. 3rd-shift Maintenance Supervisor. JV with CHS (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chs-consumer-supply-distributing-form-livestock-feed-venture-139226284.html).

**Trigger**
- Dec 24, 2025 Salmonella recall of both biscuit lines (lot 40906513, best-by 09/06/2026) from FDA sampling; "ceased production and distribution of the lot" (https://www.petfoodindustry.com/safety-quality/pet-food-recalls/news/15774880/consumers-supply-recalls-dog-biscuits-for-salmonella-risk). FDA has since terminated the recall.

**People**
- David Patee, Owner; Dan Patee, Vice President (https://www.linkedin.com/in/dan-patee-6218bb134); Keith Rees, National Sales and Marketing Director, Companion Animal Products (https://www.linkedin.com/in/keith-rees-02337710/).

**Leadership statements**
- None found beyond recall notice; unknown.

**Proposed give**
- US-made replacement biscuit program (certifications only as confirmed on the facility data sheet): match Original Meaty and Meaty w/ Chicken formulas in 4 lb and 20 lb, with lot-level Salmonella testing/COA and sampling without a production backlog.

**Risks**
- May self-manufacture (two plants); recall completed, urgency fading; feed-channel price points; CHS JV governance.
