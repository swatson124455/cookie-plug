## Mightylicious Gluten Free

**Product line**
- Seven ready-to-eat GF cookie varieties incl. vegan/vegetarian; flavors seen: Brown Butter Chocolate Chip, Vegan Chocolate Chip, Oatmeal Raisin, Oatmeal Coconut, Salted Peanut Butter, Shortbread, Double Dutch (https://www.delimarketnews.com/quick-bite/mightylicious-gluten-free-expands-wholesale-access-faire-retailers-nationwide-carolyn-haeler-comments/deli-staff/fri-04032026-0826/21257; Amazon listings e.g. https://www.amazon.com/Mightylicious-Brown-Butter-Chocolate-Chip-Cookies/dp/B0D7C44BHP).
- 6.5 oz bag, 9 cookies; individually wrapped; reformulated "thicker, crispier" line shipping June 2026 (https://www.nosh.com/pr/2026/03/11/mightylicious-gluten-free-cookies-debut-new-thicker-crispier-even-more-delicious-sweetness-coming-to-consumers-june-2026).
- Also GF all-purpose flour, vegan flour, vegan brownie mix (https://www.nosh.com/pr/2026/09/24/mightylicious-gluten-free-launches-capital-raise-on-republic-to-fuel-national-retail-expansion-and-supply-chain-integration).
- Certified GF, Kof-K Kosher Dairy (shared equipment), Non-GMO, vegan options; shelf-stable, preservative-free (https://mightylicious.com/pages/faqs).

**Where sold**
- ~1,100 stores; Walmart, Costco, Whole Foods, Kroger, Fairway, King's, Price Chopper; Amazon, DTC, Faire wholesale (https://njbiz.com/nj-gluten-free-cookie-brand-mightylicious-expands-nationwide/; https://commercialbaking.com/online-wholesaler-broadens-mightylicious-distribution/).

**Who makes it today**
- "Small batch, hand-crafted in New Jersey" in a facility that also runs peanuts/tree nuts/soy on shared lines (https://mightylicious.com/pages/faqs). 2021 founder note: "her co-packer told her they would have to delay an order" (https://republic.com/insights/founders/carolyn-potz-mighty-monkey). Co-packer name: unknown.

**Scale signals**
- $5M from 4,365 investors on Republic (2022); Q1 2021 revenue +514% YoY, projected $2.2M (https://republic.com/mighty-monkey; https://kingscrowd.com/mighty-monkey-on-republic-2022/). Aug 2026: ~12 new chains (https://njbiz.com/nj-gluten-free-cookie-brand-mightylicious-expands-nationwide/). 2026 Form C filed (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002147043/000214704326000002/mighty.pdf). Raise to "expand its team and onboard leadership executives." Headcount: unknown.

**Trigger**
- Sept 24, 2026: Republic raise for Wakefern + Wegmans entry, Amazon DTC, and "full vertical integration of its supply chain" (https://www.nosh.com/pr/2026/09/24/mightylicious-gluten-free-launches-capital-raise-on-republic-to-fuel-national-retail-expansion-and-supply-chain-integration).

**People**
- Carolyn Haeler (aka Carolyn Potz), Founder and CEO (https://www.linkedin.com/in/cpotz/). Ops/sales leads: unknown.

**Founder statements**
- "building the infrastructure to serve gluten-free consumers at a national scale while protecting our margins" (NOSH, Sept 2026). Podcasts: Witness Business ep. 93, Mogil's Mobcast ep. 101 (https://www.witnessbusiness.com/podcast/episode-0093-carolyn-haeler).

**Proposed give**
- Benchmark sample of the Brown Butter Chocolate Chip in the new thick/crispy spec, run in a nut-free environment (removes the FAQ cross-contamination caveat), plus a Wakefern/Wegmans volume-readiness plan.

**Risks**
- "Full vertical integration" may mean an owned plant, not a co-man. Raise not yet closed.
