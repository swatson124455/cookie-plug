**Product line**

- Magic Bars (8-pack), Magic Toppers, InstaBites (fresh shelf-stable), LuxBites, GreenBites (fresh/frozen), SuperSalad six-pack (Nov 2025); 16 SKUs as of July 2025 (https://www.amazon.com/PawCo-Magic-Bar-Joint-Treat/dp/B0GDRKLV6F, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pawco-turns-4-with-bold-expansion-plans-to-lead-the-future-of-dog-nutrition-302512790.html, https://www.petfoodindustry.com/news-newsletters/pet-food-news/news/15771685/pawco-pawco-launches-supersalad-formulated-for-canines).
- Claims: Pet Sustainability Coalition accreditation; "2024 Dog Food Organic Product of the Year" (https://www.nutritioninsight.com/news/pawco-magic-cookie-functional-dog-treats-ai.html).

**Where sold**
- DTC, Amazon (top-3 Fresh Dog Food), vet clinics, Chewy since June 2026 (https://www.petage.com/science-backed-dog-nutrition-pawco-retail-chewy/, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pawco-brand-refresh-fuels-rapid-growth-challenging-legacy-pet-food-brands-302696492.html).

**Who makes it today**

- "80,000-square-foot pilot plant"; Magic Cookies "hand-crafted and baked — they never touch machines" (https://www.petfoodprocessing.net/articles/19599-pawco-introduces-magic-cookies).

**Scale signals**

- $1M ARR, 1000% YoY, team +27% by July 2024 (https://vegconomist.com/pet-food/pawco-foods-1000-growth-achieves-1m-annual-recurring-revenue-plant-based-pet-food/). 11-50 employees (https://www.linkedin.com/company/pawco-foods).
- 20%+ sales lift after Feb 2026 rebrand; open reqs for VP Operations, VP Growth (https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/vp-of-operations-%E2%80%93-pawco-at-pawco-foods-4198700920).

**Trigger**
- June 2026 Chewy launch including Magic Cookies, a hand-made SKU (https://www.petage.com/science-backed-dog-nutrition-pawco-retail-chewy/).

**People**
- Dr. Mahsa Vazin, Founder & CEO. Others: unknown (VP Ops/Growth roles posted, names not shown).

**Founder statements**
- "Launching on Chewy represents an exciting next chapter" (petage URL above); "this is just the beginning of our journey" (vegconomist URL above).

**Proposed give**
- Automated bake of Magic Cookie to free the hand-craft pilot line, plus a human-and-dog peanut-butter/apple cookie pairing for gifting. Angle on Vazin's Impossible R&D background: formulation-to-shelf.

**Risks**
