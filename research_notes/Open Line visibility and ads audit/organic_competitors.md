# Organic search competitors and gap analysis for co-packing buyer queries (US, September 2026)

Method note, which applies to every section: the results below come from the WebSearch tool (US-only), run on 2026-09-30. **These are not Google rankings.** The tool returns about 10 links per query. Its order and composition may differ from a live google.com SERP, and it does not show SERP features: People Also Ask (PAA), local pack, AI Overview, video carousels or ads. Direct page fetches of partnerslate.com and theunderbite.co were blocked by the network proxy, so content depth for those two pages is judged from titles and search snippets only. No search volumes, keyword difficulty or CPCs were checked; all of those are "not verifiable" here. Some target queries were merged to stay within the tool budget. "dog treat co-packer" and "dog treat manufacturer private label" were run as "dog treat co-packer private label" and "private label dog treats manufacturer USA". "co-packer with low minimums" and "small batch co-packer" were run as "small batch co-packer low minimums". "bakery co-packer" was covered by "baked goods co-packer" and the cookie queries, which both returned PartnerSlate /bakery/.

## Who ranks on page one for each query

### Takeaway
Page one is split three ways. Directories and lists (pickyourown.org, specialtyfoodcopackers.com, mrcheckout.net, state and extension lists, ensun.io, Keychain) hold the cookie, bakery and "near me" queries. Individual manufacturers' service pages hold the dog-treat and pet-treat queries. Blog guides from co-packers, SaaS tools and consultancies hold the informational queries (MOQ, cost, how to find, commissary). PartnerSlate appears for the bakery and pet-food head terms. No Reddit thread surfaced in any result set.

## Strongest competitors and why; which queries look winnable

### Takeaway
The strongest incumbents are marketplaces and aggregators with domain authority and scale: PartnerSlate, Keychain, pickyourown.org and ensun.io. Behind them come long-established manufacturers with dedicated category pages (YouBar, Gold Medal, Toufayan, PetDine). The most winnable queries for a new domain are the informational and long-tail ones where the ranking pages are generic, beverage-focused, forum threads or old: MOQ, cost per unit, small batch or low minimums, commissary to co-packer, shelf-stable cookies, and pet food co-packer.

### Cited Findings
- PartnerSlate: one page per category (bakery, pet food) plus a Learning Center that ranks two near-duplicate "what is a co-packer" URLs for the MOQ query. It advertises network scale ("553 bakery manufacturers") — [PartnerSlate bakery](https://partnerslate.com/bakery/), [PartnerSlate LC](https://partnerslate.com/learningcenter/what-is-a-co-packer/), [PartnerSlate LC 2](https://partnerslate.com/learningcenter/what-is-a-copacker/)
- Keychain: programmatic listicles across many cookie subtypes and geographies, plus blog content ("Co-Packing Challenges") that ranks for MOQ — [Keychain](https://www.keychain.com/manufacturer/private-label-cookies), [Keychain blog](https://www.keychain.com/k/b/co-packing-challenges)
- pickyourown.org: a master list plus state and category lists, and the dominant result for "near me" — [pickyourown](https://pickyourown.org/list_of_copackers.htm)
- ensun.io and usetorg.com: "Top 100 / 15 Best … (2026)" list pages with the year in the title, ranking for private-label dog treats — [ensun](https://ensun.io/search/private-label-dog-treat), [usetorg](https://usetorg.com/category/private-label-dog-treats)
- Signs of staleness or weak pages among ranking results: PetDine ranks a URL literally named "services-old", and Not Just Cookies ranks a page slugged "copy-of-corporate-ordering" — [PetDine](https://www.petdinellc.com/services-old/all-natural-dog-treat-manufacturing/), [Not Just Cookies](https://notjustcookies.com/pages/copy-of-corporate-ordering)
- The Packaging World and B&W Packaging pages that rank for "pet food co-packer" cite 2019–2024 market data, and the B&W post is dated 2021 — [Packworld](https://www.packworld.com/trends/contract-packaging/article/21927766/pet-food-copackers-are-indemand), [B&W](https://www.bwpackaging.com/blog/post/blog/2021/09/10/planning-capital-costs-in-pet-food-packaging)
- Forums rank for small-batch or low-minimum queries (TheHotPepper, BBQ-Brethren), a common sign of thin coverage — [TheHotPepper](https://thehotpepper.com/threads/im-a-small-batch-low-minimum-order-copacker-ask-me-anything.77523/)
- The MOQ results are beverage-heavy (RapidCPG, Power Brands, 3 Brothers Winery) — [RapidCPG](https://rapidcpg.com/blog/beverage-minimum-order-quantities), [Power Brands](https://www.powerbrands.us/beverage-co-packer)
- The cost-per-unit results come from contract packagers pricing repack or kitting work, not food manufacturing — [Industrial Packaging](https://www.industrialpackaging.com/blog/contract-packaging-cost)

### Inferences
- **Hardest (low priority for a new domain in year one):** "cookie co-packer", "bakery co-packer", "baked goods co-packer" and "cookie co-packers near me". These are held by aged directories, government and university pages, PartnerSlate, and manufacturers with years-old category pages. "Near me" likely also triggers a local pack, where a non-facility referral site has no physical listing to show (not verified).
- **Moderate:** "dog treat co-packer", "private label dog treats" and "pet treat co-manufacturer". Manufacturer pages here are often thin product-and-contact pages, with year-stamped listicle aggregators. A detailed dog-treat page with an FAQ could compete, but manufacturers with real facility addresses and certifications have a trust edge.
- **Most winnable:**
  - "co-packer minimum order quantity" (bakery- and pet-specific angle).
  - "co-packer cost per unit" (manufacturing, not repack, cost build-up).
  - "small batch co-packer" and "co-packer with low minimums" (no bakery or pet page ranks; forums rank).
  - "commissary kitchen to co-packer" (ranking pages are kitchen-rental operators with a bias toward staying in the kitchen).
  - "shelf stable cookies co-packer" (directories plus one manufacturer).
  - "pet food co-packer" (publishers and old articles; few service pages).
  - This fits the existing guides "co-packer minimums, lead times and costs", "when to leave the commissary" and "refrigerated to shelf-stable".
- Backlink counts, domain age and authority metrics were not checked. The assessments above rest on SERP composition only.

### Gaps
- No backlink or domain-authority data (Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz were not accessible). Strength here is inferred from how often a domain appears and its type, not from link metrics.
- Content depth of the PartnerSlate and The Underbite pages could not be confirmed because fetches were blocked. Reviews and ratings on competitor pages were not checked.

## Long-tail topics with no strong page (content gaps vs. our guides)

No bakery- or pet-specific page ranks for the MOQ, cost, small-batch or questions-to-ask intents. Cost pages quote repack prices, not bake or extrude costs. The existing Open Line guides already cover MOQ, lead times and costs, commissary, shelf-stable, and how to find cookie and dog-treat co-packers. Remaining gaps include: questions to ask or co-packer agreement and IP; pet-food-specific sourcing, since there is no pet food guide; small-batch or pilot runs; and private-label (retailer-brand) versus co-packing.

## Prioritized on-page and technical recommendations

### Takeaway
Don't attack the directory-held head terms first. Win the informational long tail with category-specific numbers and checklists, and make the four line pages the most complete single answer for their category. That means clear capability facts, an FAQ, a quote-intake checklist, and internal links from every guide. Build authority through listings on the directories and marketplaces that already rank.

### Cited Findings (evidence the recommendations rest on)
- Directories that rank on page one accept or list co-packers: pickyourown.org, specialtyfoodcopackers.com, the mass.gov, Minnesota MDA, Penn State and Cornell lists, and Keychain and PartnerSlate profiles — [pickyourown](https://pickyourown.org/list_of_copackers.htm), [MDA](https://www.mda.state.mn.us/minnesota-co-packer-directory), [Penn State](https://extension.psu.edu/list-of-co-packers-in-pennsylvania-and-beyond), [Keychain](https://www.keychain.com/manufacturer/private-label-cookies), [PartnerSlate](https://partnerslate.com/bakery/)
- Ranking list pages put the year in the title ("Top 100 … in 2026", "15 Best … (2026)", "Co-Packing Pricing 2026", "2025 Guide") — [ensun](https://ensun.io/search/private-label-dog-treat), [usetorg](https://usetorg.com/category/private-label-dog-treats), [MSL](https://msl-indy.com/co-packing-pricing/), [Buske](https://www.buske.com/blog/co-packing-companies-strategies-for-selecting-a-provider)
- Manufacturers often hold two slots with separate co-packing and private-label pages (Gold Medal, YouBar, PetDine) — [Gold Medal](https://www.goldmedalbakery.com/services/private-label/)
- Snippets that surface concrete numbers get quoted in search summaries: "553 bakery manufacturers", "pilot run MOQs starting at 500 LBs", "shelf life of 9 months" — [PartnerSlate](https://partnerslate.com/bakery/), [specialtyfoodcopackers](https://specialtyfoodcopackers.com/Cookies.html)

### Inferences (prioritized)
1. **Answer-first numbers on MOQ, lead-time and cost pages.** Open each guide with a two- or three-sentence direct answer giving ranges (sourced industry ranges, clearly labeled). Then give category-specific tables for cookies, baked goods, dog treats and pet food. Keep facility-specific numbers out until confirmed (`TO_CONFIRM` rule). This targets the most winnable queries and matches AI Overview extraction patterns.
2. **Add the missing guides** from the gap list: questions to ask or co-packer agreement, pet food co-packer, small-batch and pilot runs, private label vs. co-packing for dog treats, and quote cost build-up. Mark each with FAQPage-eligible Q&A blocks and link each one to the matching line page.
3. **Split intent on line pages to earn two slots where competitors do.** For example, add a private-label dog treats section or a sibling page, alongside /dog-treat-co-packer/. Consider "shelf-stable / individually wrapped cookies" as an H2 or a subpage of /cookie-co-packer/.
4. **Year-stamped, visibly dated content.** Show "Updated September 2026" on guides and use `dateModified` in the Article JSON-LD. Several ranking competitors show years in titles, and some ranking pages are stale (2021 articles, "services-old" URLs).
5. **Directory and marketplace listings as backlinks and referral traffic.** Submit to pickyourown.org, specialtyfoodcopackers.com, and the state agriculture and extension co-packer lists (mass.gov, MN MDA, Penn State, Cornell, OSU, where eligible for a referral partner rather than a facility). Keychain and PartnerSlate listings require the manufacturer's participation, so confirm with the owner. Don't list the facility name publicly unless approved.
6. **Local "near me" is not the fight.** A referral site cannot hold a Google Business Profile for a facility it doesn't operate. Target state-modified phrases only if the owner confirms the facility region can be named (not recommended now).
7. **Trust signals competitors show:** certifications, formula-ownership statement, NDA offer, and the process from sample to production. Publish only confirmed facts from `config/facility.yaml`.
8. **Technical:** the site's static, JavaScript-free build, JSON-LD and llms.txt already exceed most ranking manufacturer pages. Examples of weak competitor pages are Shopify "pages/copy-of-…" slugs and old URL paths. Make sure of the following:
   - canonical tags, an XML sitemap and a Search Console submission at launch;
   - unique titles containing the exact head term, for example "Cookie Co-Packer for Emerging Brands | Open Line";
   - BreadcrumbList on every guide;
   - internal links from the FAQ to guides;
   - no thin duplicate pages (PartnerSlate's twin URLs show how easy that mistake is to make).
9. **Watch Reddit.** No Reddit thread surfaced, so there is no forum SERP to hijack right now. Recheck quarterly in a live Google SERP, since Reddit visibility changes often.

### Gaps
